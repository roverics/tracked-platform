# R1 Requirements and Planning

Status: **planning only**. R1 is a future full-size, production-intent tracked platform. It has not been represented here as fabricated, tested, manufactured, or load-rated.

P0 results are inputs to R1 requirements; they are not automatically scalable proof. In particular, no validated 100 kg capacity is currently documented.

## Requirement method

Each requirement should eventually include an ID, measurable statement, rationale/source, verification method, owner, and status. Use `TBD` instead of guessing.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1-SYS-001 | Intended use | TBD after use cases and operating environment are agreed | Product planning | Review | Open |

| R1-SVC-001 | Serviceability | TBD access, replaceable modules, tools, and maintenance intervals | Lifecycle planning | Demonstration | Open |

## Inputs needed before baselining

- prioritized customer/use cases;
- terrain and environmental definitions;
- payload versus vehicle mass terminology and targets;
- legal, transport, and safety constraints for intended markets;
- manufacturing volume and cost assumptions;
- P0 drivetrain, track, thermal, power, and control evidence; and
- subsystem interface definitions with measurable acceptance criteria.

A requirement is not “validated” until its stated verification is completed against an identified R1 configuration.
