# Roadmap

This roadmap is evidence-gated. Dates and performance targets should be added only when their assumptions and owners are known.

## P0 learning sequence

1. **Recover and baseline existing work** — add owned editable CAD, record dimensions and chain measurement method, assign part IDs/revisions, and document third-party provenance.
2. **Static running-gear fit** — record track articulation, sprocket engagement, alignment, tension range, and observed interference.
3. **Powered, instrumented single-track module** — operate one track lane while recording electrical, thermal, and mechanical observations. This is the next milestone.
4. **Repeatable bench envelope** — vary speed, tension, surface, duration, and measured load with explicit acceptance criteria.
5. **Two-track P0 integration** — integrate power, controls, enclosure, and emergency stop behavior; document configuration and failures.
6. **P0 field learning** — conduct controlled mobility trials and close the most important R1 requirement gaps.

## R1 planning gate

R1 design begins from reviewed requirements and P0 evidence, not by scaling P0 geometry. See [r1-requirements.md](r1-requirements.md). Potential application configurations remain product exploration until platform interfaces and requirements justify separate work.
